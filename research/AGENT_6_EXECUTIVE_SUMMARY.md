### What "Model-Less" Means Technically

**NOT**: "Zero models, pure chaos"

**YES**: "No boundaries between models"

**Implementation**:
- **Shared activation space**: All 4 forms operate in same latent representation
- **Dynamic compute allocation**: "GR00T needs 80% now, Qwen3 20%" changes per-token
- **Unified embedding layer**: Vision/language/action/reasoning all → same tokens
- **Continuous learning**: Weight updates affect entire substrate, not isolated models
- **Hierarchical routing**: Tokens activate only necessary awareness forms (skip others)
